How signals work

Methodology

TrendRadar monitors public web and search signals, then ranks changes in attention to help creators and marketers investigate early opportunities.

1. Collect

We collect public Google Trends and GDELT signals. Some additional opportunities may be discovered through grounded web research, with their source links shown in the product when available.

2. Measure momentum

We compare recent observations to earlier observations. Velocity is a directional indicator of changing attention, not a verified audience total.

3. Explain and verify

Every signal should be checked at its original source before action. A score is a prioritisation aid, not a prediction or recommendation.
